<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Final Statement</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">&#8216;When I was detained, all my electronic devices were seized during the search. If there had been anything on them, I think it would have been included in the case file.&#8217;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your Honour, I fully support my lawyers. I believe that my guilt has not been proven. When I was detained, all my electronic devices were seized during the search. If there had been anything on them, I think it would have been included in the case file. But nothing was found because there was nothing in the case file. And I state that I have nothing to do with these videos. I do not know Bolot Temirov.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I saw these videos for the first time in my life yesterday, just as you did. I have nothing to do with them: I did not edit them, put them together, publish them, or write a script for them–I am not involved with them in any way.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">I ask you, Your Honour, to take these circumstances into account, make a fair decision, and deliver a just verdict.</p>
