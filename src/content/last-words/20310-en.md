<h2 class="wp-block-heading">The Final Statement</h2>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading">&#8216;I ask you to acquit us all.&#8217;</h3>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"></p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your Honour, I fully support the position of my defence. We had no intention of committing any crimes. Our communication in the chat was entirely ordinary.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In view of this, I consider the prosecutor’s demands that we be sentenced to be baseless. This is especially true of Ruslan, Pyotr and Vyacheslav, for whom the prosecutor has requested actual prison sentences.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Your Honour, I ask you to acquit us all.</p>
